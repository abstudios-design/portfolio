02 / Care experience

Life Bridge

A more connected care experience for seniors, families and the people supporting them.

RoleUX / UI designerFocusService design, interactionTypeHealthcare platform
Care, connected.

The brief

Bring the whole care circle closer.

Life Bridge makes everyday care easier to coordinate. It gives families a shared view of routines, appointments and updates while keeping the experience gentle for older adults.

The design work focused on reducing uncertainty: clear status, plain language and thoughtful handoffs make the platform feel supportive rather than clinical.
