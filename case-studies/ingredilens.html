Overview

IngredLens is a personalized food and beverage scanning application designed to help people make safer and healthier purchasing decisions. Users can scan packaged food products to instantly understand their ingredients, nutritional values, allergens, and potential health risks. By considering a user's medical conditions, allergies, and dietary preferences, IngredLens transforms complex food labels into simple, personalized guidance:

Safe. Caution. Avoid.

The goal was to design an experience that answers one simple but important question:

“Is this product safe for me?”

The Challenge

Food packaging contains a large amount of nutritional and ingredient information, but understanding what that information means can be difficult. A product that appears healthy may contain ingredients that are unsuitable for someone with:

Diabetes High blood pressure Thyroid conditions Food allergies High cholesterol Specific dietary preferences

For example, a peanut butter product may look like a healthy choice. However, a person managing diabetes may need to understand whether its sugar and carbohydrate content could significantly impact their blood sugar.

The challenge was to create a product-scanning experience that could:

  • Simplify complex nutritional information
  • Personalize results for every user
  • Clearly communicate health risks
  • Avoid overwhelming users with medical terminology
  • Help users make better decisions immediately
  • Recommend healthier alternatives when necessary

The Problem

Food labels provide information, but they don't provide personalized answers.

Users often need to manually read ingredients, understand nutritional tables, identify allergens, and determine whether a product aligns with their health requirements.

Time-consuming Confusing Difficult for non-experts Prone to incorrect interpretation

The key UX problem became:

How might we help users understand whether a food product is suitable for their personal health profile within seconds of scanning it?

02 — Personalized Registration

The registration experience collects key details to understand the user better and enable personalized insights. The app also calculates BMI automatically and offers a guest option for a seamless start.

KEY ELEMENTS

Basic Information

We collect essential details like name,age, gender, height, and weight to personalize the experience.

Auto BMI Calculation

The app automatically calculates the user's BMI and presents the result in a simple, easy-to-understand format.

Why BMI?

BMI provides an additional data point that can support broader health and calorie-related personalization. However, we present it as an informative indicator, not the sole definition of a user's health.

Guest Option

A prominent "Skip & Continue as Guest" option ensures users can immediately access the core scanning feature without unnecessary friction.

03 — Medical Conditions & Allergies

Personalized for Your Health Needs

This screen enables personalization without making the experience feel like a complicated medical form.

KEY ELEMENTS

Select Your Conditions

Choose from common conditions like Diabetes, High BP, Thyroid, PCOS, Cholesterol, Heart Disease, and Kidney Issues.

Add Allergies

Quick-select tags for common allergens like Peanut, Gluten, Dairy, Soy, and Shellfish.

Faster with Chips

We used selectable chips and tags instead of traditional checkboxes to make profile creation faster and more mobile-friendly.

More Relevant Results

Your selections help us provide personalized ingredient analysis and healthier recommendations.
