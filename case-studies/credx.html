01 / Overview

A private, policy-driven expense ecosystem for enterprises.

Managing employee expenses can become complicated quickly. Employees need a simple way to submit and track expenses, while managers, finance teams and administrators need approvals, policy enforcement and reporting.

CredX bridges those needs in one centralized environment. The platform manages approved expenses such as business travel, meals, corporate events, client meetings, transportation and accommodation.

Private by designExpose sensitive information only when a role needs it.
Policy-drivenBring limits and requirements into the workflow.
Role-basedDesign around employees, managers, finance and admins.
Action-orientedMake dashboards answer what needs attention.

02 / The challenge

How do you create transparency without compromising privacy?

CLIENT REQUIREMENTEmployee and payment information must remain highly confidential.

04 / The solution

Four principles shaped the product.

01

Privacy by Design

Sensitive employee and payment information is exposed only to authorized users through role-based access and progressive disclosure.

02

Policy-Driven Spending

Eligibility, limits, required documents and approval workflows appear at the moment they matter.

03

Role-Based Experiences

Employees, managers, finance and administrators receive different experiences instead of one overloaded dashboard.

04

Intelligent Information Hierarchy

The UI prioritizes “What do I need to know?” before “What information is available?”

06 / Understanding the user

Four users. Four different jobs.

01

Employee

Goal: Submit and track expenses quickly.
  • Is this expense allowed?
  • How much can I spend?
  • What documentation is required?
  • Has my expense been approved?
  • When will reimbursement happen?
02

Manager

Goal: Approve legitimate expenses quickly.
  • Is this within policy?
  • Does approval need attention?
  • What information can I review?
03

Finance

Goal: Maintain financial control.
  • How much is the organization spending?
  • Which categories are increasing?
  • Which expenses require review?
04

Administrator

Goal: Control the expense ecosystem.
  • Which policies are active?
  • Who can access what?
  • What are the spending limits?
  • Which workflows are configured?

07 / Key user pain points

Designing around the moments that create friction.

01Policy uncertaintyEmployees don't know whether an expense is eligible before spending.
02Complex approvalsMultiple approval layers can make expense management slow.
03Excessive informationToo much financial or employee information creates privacy concerns.
04Manual trackingTeams spend unnecessary time following requests.
05Poor status visibilityDraft → Submitted → Under Review → Approved → Reimbursed.
06Lack of personalizationDifferent roles require different information.

Final takeaway

CredX wasn't designed to expose more financial information.

It was designed to expose the right information to the right person at the right time.

Simple for employees. Powerful for finance. Private by design.
