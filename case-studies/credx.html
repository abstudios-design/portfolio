05 / Fintech experience

CredX

A smarter spending experience that makes financial decisions feel lighter and more visible.

RoleProduct designerFocusInformation architecture, UITypePersonal finance product
Spend with intent.

The brief

Make the everyday money story clearer.

CredX helps people see their spending as a set of understandable choices rather than a stream of transactions. The experience makes patterns, limits and next steps easier to find.

A bright visual language and carefully prioritized summaries turn financial information into something people can return to without friction.
