06 / Employee experience

Life at Zenesys

An employee experience designed to make work, culture and belonging easier to navigate.

RoleUX / UI designerFocusContent, interaction, systemsTypeInternal people platform
Work, with humanity.

The brief

Put people at the center of the workplace.

Life at Zenesys gives employees a shared place to find resources, connect with colleagues and understand the culture around their work.

The design balances useful utility with warmth: content is easier to discover, key moments feel more personal and the system supports a growing organization without losing its human tone.
