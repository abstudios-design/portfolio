Life at Zenesys

A unified employee experience platform designed to simplify everyday HR operations.

RoleUI/UX DesignerProductAI-assisted HRMSAudienceEmployees + HR/AdminFocusUX · UI · Systems · Responsive

One connected workplace platform for onboarding, attendance, leave, payroll, employee records, documents and exit formalities.

01 / Executive summary

One HR ecosystem, built around the people using it.

Life at Zenesys was designed as a centralized HRMS to reduce the friction of managing everyday employee processes.

The platform serves two primary audiences: Employees, who need quick access to attendance, leave, payroll, documents, profile information and workplace services; and HR/Admin teams, who manage employee records, approvals, payroll, onboarding, documents, attendance and exit processes.

Instead of treating HR functions as separate modules, the experience connects them through a consistent navigation system, shared design language and role-based workflows.

Simplify

Make common HR tasks easy to understand and complete.

Centralize

Bring employee information, documents and HR operations into one platform.

Reduce cognitive load

Use clear hierarchy, progressive disclosure and familiar interaction patterns.

Support both roles

Create tailored experiences for employees and administrators.

Every device

Provide dedicated desktop and mobile experiences.

02 / Company background

A little less admin. A lot more possibility.

Zenesys is a technology services and digital engineering company working across CMS, CRM, web, mobile, UI/UX and emerging AI-led solutions.

As organizations grow, internal HR operations can become fragmented across spreadsheets, emails, documents and disconnected tools.

Give employees a simpler workday while giving HR teams a more structured way to manage people and processes.

03 / The challenge

Make disconnected HR processes feel like one system.

HR processes often involve multiple stakeholders, documents, approvals and deadlines.

01

Employee

Check attendance → Apply leave → Track approval → View payroll → Download documents.

02

HR / Admin

Create employee → Verify documents → Manage attendance → Approve leave → Process payroll → Complete exit.

The design challenge was to create one HR ecosystem that could accommodate different roles, large amounts of employee information, operational workflows, sensitive documents, approval-based processes, payroll information, desktop/mobile usage and growing HR requirements.

04 / The problem

The information existed. Finding and understanding it was the friction.

For Employees

Everyday questions needed quick, predictable answers.

  • How many leaves do I have?
  • Did my attendance get recorded?
  • What is my salary information?
  • Where are my documents?
  • What is the status of my request?
  • What information does HR have about me?
Speed + clarity + discoverability

For HR / Admin

A significantly more complex information ecosystem needed control without overload.

  • Employee records
  • Attendance
  • Leave approvals
  • Payroll
  • Documents
  • Onboarding and exit formalities
  • Organizational information
Information density + control + visibility + efficiency

05 / The solution

Two role-based experiences, one connected system.

Employee Experience

A streamlined workspace focused on frequent employee tasks.

Dashboard → Attendance → Leave → Payroll → Documents → Employee Profile

HR / Admin Experience

A more information-dense operational workspace.

Dashboard → Employees → Attendance → Leave → Payroll → Documents → Onboarding → Exit

Components

Navigation: sidebar, mobile navigation, breadcrumbs, active states.

Forms: text, password, select, form groups, upload fields.

Actions: primary, secondary, text and contextual actions.

Data & feedback

Tables, data rows, record cards, summary metrics, status badges, approval states, security messages and informational messages.

Utilities include search, notifications, profile/avatar and secure access indicators.

Responsive design

Not a smaller desktop.

Desktop: Sidebar + Header + Large Content Workspace, providing the space required for tables and information-dense HR operations.

Mobile: Compact Header + Stacked Content + Mobile Navigation. Tables become cards where necessary, content becomes vertically structured, and actions remain thumb-accessible.

Design philosophy

Five principles keep the system human and usable.

01 — People first

HR software should feel human rather than administrative.

02 — Clarity over complexity

Complex HR processes should feel simple to the user.

03 — Progressive disclosure

Show the right information at the right level.

04 — Consistency creates confidence

Repeated patterns make enterprise software easier to learn.

05 — Mobile is a first-class experience

Employees shouldn't lose functionality simply because they are away from their desktop.

Final case study statement

Life at Zenesys transforms HR from a collection of administrative processes into a connected employee experience.

By combining role-based workflows, structured information architecture, reusable components and responsive experiences, the platform creates a simpler experience for employees while giving HR teams the visibility and control needed to manage the complete employee lifecycle.

Life at Zenesys — A role-based, responsive HRMS designed to simplify employee self-service and streamline HR operations across the complete employee lifecycle.
