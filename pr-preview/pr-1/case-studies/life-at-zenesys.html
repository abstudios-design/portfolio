01 / The challenge

HR should feel less like paperwork.

The brief called for a complete responsive HRMS for Zenesys employees and HR/Admin teams — a single product covering everyday employee needs as well as operational HR workflows.

The product needed to feel premium, modern and enterprise-ready while remaining data-rich without becoming visually overwhelming.

People + Technology + AI + Simplicity

The central product idea: make the digital workplace easier to understand, faster to navigate and more useful for both sides of HR.

02 / Product scope

One system. Two experiences.

Employee

A focused self-service experience for everyday workplace tasks.

  • Attendance and working hours
  • Leave balances, requests and history
  • Salary and payslips
  • Onboarding and exit documents
  • Profile and notifications
  • Help, support and AI assistance

Admin / HR

A control centre for people operations, approvals and workforce data.

  • Employee directory and registration
  • Attendance and leave management
  • Payroll and salary processing
  • Onboarding and exit management
  • Reports, analytics and documents
  • Policy, holiday and system settings

05 / Admin experience

A command centre for people operations.

The HR experience is deliberately more information-dense, but still follows the same hierarchy: overview first, decisions second, detail when needed.

Workforce visibility

KPI cards, employee directory, headcount and attendance analytics create a shared operational picture.

Fast approvals

Leave requests expose duration, reason and employee history before an approve/reject decision.

Payroll control

A defined processing flow connects attendance, leave, salary, deductions, statutory items, review and approval.

06 / AI interaction

Meet Zenesys AI — the shortcut through HR.

The AI assistant is available throughout the platform as a contextual layer. Its role is to reduce repetitive HR tasks and help users find information faster, with responses constrained by role and permissions.

08 / Responsive by intent

Not a smaller desktop.

The experience is designed around the context of each device. Desktop remains the primary enterprise workspace; tablet adapts the navigation and grids; mobile prioritises bottom navigation, collapsible sections, responsive cards, mobile-friendly tables and full-screen forms.

This keeps critical actions such as check-in, leave requests and AI assistance usable when employees are away from their desks.

09 / What I designed

From the workplace model to the interaction details.

The product specification covers authentication, employee and admin information architecture, dashboards, attendance, leave, payroll, onboarding, exit workflows, notifications, AI interaction and responsive behaviour.

The resulting design system is intentionally restrained: rounded surfaces, clear status colours, professional data tables, elegant charts, consistent spacing and a single strong action hierarchy.
